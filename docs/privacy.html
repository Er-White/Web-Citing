隐私政策

Web Citing · 生效日期：2026-09-10 · 版本 1.0.0

一句话概括：本扩展不收集、不上传、不存储任何用户数据。 所有处理都在你的浏览器本地完成，扩展没有任何联网功能。

1. 我们不收集什么

本扩展没有服务器，源码中不存在任何网络请求代码。具体而言：

  • 不上传、不转发、不备份任何数据；
  • 不含统计分析、埋点、崩溃上报、广告或任何第三方 SDK；
  • 不读取、不记录你的浏览历史；
  • 不创建账号，不要求登录，不索取个人信息。

2. 扩展会读取什么

仅当你主动点击扩展图标或在页面上选择「引用本页」时，扩展才会读取当前标签页的公开元数据，用于生成引用：

  • 页面标题、作者、发布日期；
  • 网站名称、网址；
  • 页面自身通过 meta 标签公开声明的上述信息。

这些信息只在你点击后的那一刻读取，处理完即用于填充面板，不会离开你的设备。 扩展不会在后台自动读取任何页面，也不会读取你没有主动点击的标签页。

页面摘要字段默认不导出；只有当你在面板中主动勾选「同时导出摘要」时，该段文字才会被写入你本地的导出文件。

3. 数据存储

扩展只在本地保存 4 项偏好设置：界面语言、引用格式、导出格式、是否包含检索日期，以及摘要开关。 这些设置通过浏览器提供的本地存储（chrome.storage.local）保存在你的设备上， 不参与浏览器账号同步，不发送到任何服务器。

卸载扩展后，这些本地设置会随扩展一并移除。

4. 权限说明

权限用途
读取当前页面（activeTab） 在你点击扩展时，读取当前页面公开的引用元数据。该权限是临时的，仅对你点击的那一个标签页生效。
网站访问权限（http://*/*、https://*/*） 可选权限，默认不授予。仅在浏览器未能临时授权读取本页时，由你在扩展面板中点击「授权访问」才会申请。授予后扩展才能在你每次点击时稳定读取网页元数据。
下载、存储、右键菜单、通知 分别用于保存导出的引用文件、记录你的偏好设置、提供右键菜单入口、以及在右键导出后给出结果提示。

5. 导出文件与著作权提示

扩展会把网页上的信息整理成引用记录并保存为本地文件。请注意：

  • 标题、作者、日期、网址等属于事实信息，用于文献引用是通行做法；
  • 摘要通常是作者的原创表达，受著作权保护。因此扩展默认不导出摘要，需要时请自行勾选；
  • 你在使用导出结果时，应遵守目标网站的服务条款及所在地区的法律规定；
  • 本扩展仅协助你记录你本人已经访问并可见的页面信息，不绕过任何付费墙、登录限制或技术保护措施。

扩展按「现状」提供，不对生成引用的准确性作担保。引用是否正确、是否适合你的投稿要求，请自行核对。

6. 政策变更

本政策如有更新，将随扩展新版本一并发布，并更新本页顶部的生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Web Citing · Effective 2026-09-10 · Version 1.0.0

In short: this extension collects no user data, transmits nothing, and stores nothing remotely. Everything happens locally in your browser. The extension has no networking code at all.

1. What we do not collect

The extension has no server, and its source contains no network requests of any kind. Specifically, it does not:

  • upload, transmit or back up any data;
  • include analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or any third-party SDK;
  • read or record your browsing history;
  • create accounts, require sign-in, or ask for personal information.

2. What the extension reads

Only when you click the extension icon or choose "Cite this page" from the right-click menu does it read the current tab's public metadata in order to build a citation:

  • page title, author and publication date;
  • site name and URL;
  • the above as declared by the page itself in its meta tags.

This is read at the moment you click, used to fill the panel, and never leaves your device. The extension never reads pages in the background, and never touches tabs you have not acted on.

The page abstract is not exported by default; that passage is written into your local export file only if you tick "Include abstract".

3. Storage

The extension saves five preferences locally: interface language, citation style, export format, whether to include a retrieval date, and the abstract toggle. These are held in the browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device only. They are not synced to your browser account and not sent anywhere. Removing the extension removes them.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy
Read the current tab (activeTab) Lets the extension read the current page's public citation metadata when you click the icon. The grant is temporary and applies only to the tab you clicked.
Site access (http://*/*, https://*/*) Optional, and not granted by default. Requested only if the browser declined to grant temporary access, via the "Grant access" button in the panel. Without it, page metadata can only be read on the temporary grant.
Downloads, storage, context menus, notifications To save the exported citation file, remember your preferences, provide the right-click entry point, and confirm the result of a right-click export.

5. Exported files and copyright

  • Titles, authors, dates and URLs are facts, and citing them is standard practice;
  • An abstract is normally the author's own expression and is protected by copyright, which is why the extension leaves it out unless you explicitly opt in;
  • You are responsible for complying with the terms of service of the sites you visit and with the law where you are;
  • The extension only helps you record information from pages you have already visited and can see. It circumvents no paywall, no login and no technical protection measure.

The extension is provided "as is", with no warranty as to the accuracy of the citations it generates. Verify them before relying on them.

6. Changes

Any update to this policy ships with a new version of the extension, and the effective date above is revised accordingly.
